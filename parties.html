Common Questions

Before you book

How big can my group be?+

Our two warehouses combine for big groups — we've hosted parties from 8 to 50+. Call with your headcount and we'll size the floor accordingly.

Do guests need to bring anything?+

Closed-toe shoes, comfortable clothes (long sleeves are a plus for Airsoft), and a signed waiver. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's signature — we email the form when you book.

Can we mix ages — say, an 8-year-old at a 10-year-old's party?+

Yes. Younger siblings can run with the Nerf bay or hang in our paint studio while the older crew plays gel blasters. We're set up for this.

How far ahead should we book?+

Saturdays book out 2–3 weeks ahead, especially in spring and fall. Weeknights are usually open with a few days' notice. Call us and we'll find a slot.

Can adults play, too?+

Absolutely — and they should. Bachelor parties, team-builds, "dad turns 40" — we've run them all. Adult groups usually go straight to Airsoft and have a blast.

Ready to lock in your date?

Pick up the phone — that's the fastest way to confirm your slot and customize the package to your group.
