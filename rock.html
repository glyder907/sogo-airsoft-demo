The Field

A real rock quarry. Played the way it's meant to be played.

The Prison

Multi-story building with the Conex stack just up the road. The hardest objective on the map — and the most defensible.

Bandit Camp

Guard shacks, makeshift cover, and a maze of shipping containers. Tight angles, fast firefights.

The Docks

Open ground meets stacked Conex. Where snipers earn their FPS allowance and squads earn theirs by moving smart.

Wooded High Ground

Real terrain — hills, trees, natural cover. Not a paintball field with bunkers bolted on.

Roads & Paths

Quarry haul roads cut between the engagement zones. Move fast or move smart — there's space for both.

Three-Story Building

Field Rules

Outdoor FPS limits

Outdoor engagement distances mean we can run hotter than the Ozark indoor arena. All limits chronograph with .20g BBs unless we tell you otherwise.

ClassFPS CapNotes
Rifleman≤ 400 FPSStandard AEG / GBBR
DMR≤ 450 FPSSemi-auto only · minimum engagement distance applies
Bolt-Action Sniper≤ 500 FPSBolt only · minimum engagement distance applies
Support / LMG≤ 400 FPSBox mag preferred
All Systems25 RPS maxRate-of-fire cap regardless of class

Eye protection

Fully-sealed, ANSI Z87.1-rated goggles required for everyone on the field. No exceptions, no mesh.

Age

Minimum age 12. Players under 18 need a parent or legal guardian's waiver signature on file.

Climbing

Stay off the steepest hills — quarry rock is loose and the dropoffs are real. We'll mark off-limits areas at briefing.

Single-fire events

Some scenario events run semi-only — including snipers, DMRs, and rifles. Check the event description before you build your loadout.

Pack List

What to bring

  • →2 liters of water minimum. More in summer. There's no fountain in the quarry.
  • →Sealed ANSI Z87.1 eye-pro. If you don't have it, rent at check-in.
  • →Sturdy boots. Loose rock and dirt — sneakers aren't enough.
  • →Layers. Quarry mornings start cold and afternoons get hot.
  • →Snacks. Lunch is $5 on-site, but a granola bar between rounds is gold.
  • →Extra mags & battery. No Best Buy at the quarry — pack what you need.
