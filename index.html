Why So Go

Built for the Ozarks since 2007

Two warehouses, one floor

8,000 sq ft of indoor combat space — two 4,000 sq ft warehouses joined by a courtyard, configurable for tight-quarters fights or big-group scenarios.

Family-first, all ages

Foam darts at four. Gel blasters at nine. Airsoft CQB at twelve. Whatever your kid is ready for, we've got the right format and the right gear.

Real safety, run by players

Every gun chronographs below 340 FPS with .20g BBs. Sealed eye-pro is mandatory. Full-face masks for under-18s. We don't cut corners on the things that matter.

Retail under one roof

Guns, gear, BBs, batteries, tech support. If you broke it on the field tonight, we've probably got the part on the shelf.

Indoor when it counts

Missouri weather doesn't get a vote. We play in the rain, the heat, and the cold — climate-controlled, every night we're open.

Outdoor option, too

When you want trees and terrain instead of tight corners, our outdoor field The Rock in Bolivar runs weekend ops on 80+ acres of working quarry.
