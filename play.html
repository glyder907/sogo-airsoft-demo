Field Rules

Safety first, always.

Eye protection

Sealed goggles required for every player on the floor. No mesh. No exceptions. Players under 18 wear a full-face paintball-style mask.

FPS limits

All guns chronograph below 340 FPS using .20g BBs before they touch the field. We chrono everything — yours included.

Waivers

Required for every player, every visit. Under 18? A parent or guardian signs. We keep them on file for the season.

Age minimums

Airsoft CQB: 12+. Gel Blasters: 9+. Nerf Wars: 4+. We move younger groups into the right format — nobody gets in over their head.

What to wear

Long sleeves, pants, gloves, closed-toe shoes. Hits sting less when you're covered.

Conduct

Call your hits. Respect the refs. Treat every gun like it's loaded — because on this floor, it is.
